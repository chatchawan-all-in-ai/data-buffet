# curated_saleout_mdt

7 cards in BigQuery dataset `curated_saleout_mdt`.

# Tables

* [curated_eom_bt](curated_eom_bt.md) - One row = the month-end sales total of one product at one Boonthavorn (BT) branch for one closed period, as reported by the chain itself.
* [curated_eom_gb](curated_eom_gb.md) - One row = the month-end sales total of one product and document type (SALE or RETURN) at one Global House (GB) branch for one period, as reported by the chain.
* [curated_eom_hp](curated_eom_hp.md) - One row = the month-end sales total of one product at one HomePro (HP) or Mega Home (MH) site for one period, as reported by the chain.
* [curated_eom_tw](curated_eom_tw.md) - One row = the month-end sales total of one product at one Thai Watsadu (TW) store for one period, as reported by the chain.
* [curated_saleout_mdt](curated_saleout_mdt.md) - One row = the net sales (or returns) of one product at one modern-trade branch on one sale date and document type, merged from every chain feed.
* [curated_saleout_monthly_mdt](curated_saleout_monthly_mdt.md) - One row = the monthly sell-out of one product at one branch of one modern-trade chain, taken from the single best source available for that chain and month.
* [curated_stock_mdt](curated_stock_mdt.md) - One row = the on-hand stock of one product at one Boonthavorn (BT) or Thai Watsadu (TW) branch on one stock date.
